1. System Architecture & Dual Roles

medEco serves two primary stakeholders with distinct permission models:

User Role Authentication Method Permissions & Access
Customer / Patient 10-digit mobile number + OTP Strict Privacy: Can ONLY access their own previous purchase history, past receipts, personal dose reminders, and search public medicine rack locations.
Pharmacy Owner / Staff Owner Password (admin123) or PIN (9999) Full Control: POS Billing, Inventory costs & profit margins, Rack assignment, Customer Directory audit, and Store Receipt archives.

2. "Where is Tablet?" Storage Locator

When a customer or doctor requests a medicine, enter the name into the instant search bar (e.g. Dolo 650, Augmentin, Pan 40, or Metformin).

Storage Coordinates Provided:
  • Rack Identifier: e.g., Rack A, Rack B, Cold Storage.
  • Shelf Level: e.g., Shelf 1, Shelf 2, indicating vertical position.
  • Box / Bin ID: e.g., Box-01, Box-04, locating the exact storage tray.
  • Storage Notes: e.g., "Store below 25°C" or "2°C - 8°C Keep Refrigerated".
  • Unit MRP & Stock: Real-time available units, batch number, and expiry date.

4. POS Billing with GST & Discounts

The POS terminal calculates itemized tax receipts according to Indian GST standards:

  • Cart Addition: Select quantities with stock deduction validation.
  • Customer Mobile Lookup: Automatically links the sale to the customer's phone number.
  • Discount Engine: Choose Percentage (e.g., 5%, 10%, 15%) or Flat (₹) off.
  • GST Breakdown: Computes Taxable Value, CGST (e.g. 6%), and SGST (e.g. 6%).
  • Tax Receipt: Thermal printer and A4 printable receipt with 1-click WhatsApp share.

5. Customer Mobile Login & Data Privacy

Strict Data Isolation Principle:

Each customer's account is uniquely keyed by their 10-digit mobile number ("One Customer, One Account"). Customers NEVER see other patients' purchase records, wholesale cost prices, or store billing terminals. They only see their own prescriptions, invoices, and dose checklists.

6. Medicine Dose Reminders & Adherence Tracker

Ensure patients complete their courses with scheduled reminders:

  • Timings: Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night with clock timings.
  • Meal Relation: Before Food, After Food, With Food, or Empty Stomach.
  • Daily Checklist: Interactive "Taken" or "Pending" checklist with daily adherence %.
  • WhatsApp Ping: Send formatted dose alerts to family members or patient phones.

7. Pharmacy Owner Portal & Customer Audit

Store owners sign in with password admin123 to access:

  • Customer Directory: Inspect any patient's previous order history, total spent, and active reminders.
  • Inventory & Costs: Maintain wholesale purchase prices, selling prices, and shelf locations.
  • Sales Receipt Archive: Audit all historical transactions.

8. Appearance & Theme Settings

medEco supports 3 distinct appearance modes via the Theme button in the navigation bar:

  • Light Mode: High contrast for daytime use in well-lit retail pharmacies.
  • Dark Mode: Soft, low-contrast dark palette for night shifts and reduced eye strain.
  • Default (System): Automatically adapts to the operating system's dark/light preference.
